Dub Siren — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Dub Siren is a sound synthesizer app. It is designed to work entirely on your device, offline. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no tracking of any kind. It does not read your contacts, files, location, camera, or microphone.

Information stored on your device

The app saves two things locally, using your browser or system's standard local storage, so your setup survives closing the app:

  • The three Mem slots — the synth settings you choose to save.
  • Your layout preference (Stack, Tabs or Split).

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site data in your browser, deletes it permanently.

Network access

The Android app declares the INTERNET permission because the system component it is built on requires it, but the app makes no network requests of its own. All sound is generated on the device with the Web Audio API, and every file it needs is bundled inside the app.

Children

The app is safe for all ages. Since it collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either.

Third parties

There are no third-party SDKs, services, or trackers in the app. No data is shared or sold, because none is gathered in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be raised at github.com/jamm87/portable-siren/issues.
