Power On

The first thing you'll see. Phones require a tap before a page is allowed to play audio, so this screen just unlocks the sound engine — nothing plays until you actually touch the plate afterwards. If you hear nothing later on, check your phone isn't on silent/mute and the volume is up.

Oscilloscope top strip

The thin strip above the touch plate. It traces the siren's live pitch while you play — flat and dim when idle, gold and moving while sounding. While Feedback is held it pulses red instead, with a small FEEDBACK ON label in the corner, so you can tell at a glance the echo is being pushed even without looking at a slider.

Touch plate

The big square. Hold your finger down to sound the siren:

  • Horizontal position — pitch, low on the left to high on the right.
  • Vertical position — sweep rate, slow near the bottom to fast near the top.

Drag around while holding to glide between them. Lift your finger and the siren stops, unless Latch is on.

Transport row

  • Latch — keeps the siren sounding without holding the plate down. Tap again to release it.
  • Stop — cuts the siren immediately, but only the dry tone: if there's echo in flight, the delay trail keeps ringing out on its own instead of being chopped dead.
  • Tap — tap it a few times at the pace you want and the RATE slider snaps to match. The button itself switches from "Tap" to showing the calculated BPM while you're tapping, and resets after a 2-second pause.
  • Feedback — drives the echo feedback to maximum while held, for a swelling dub throw. Release it and feedback returns to wherever the REPEATS slider is set. The oscilloscope pulses red the whole time it's held (see above).

Siren

  • Wave — the oscillator's waveform: sine (smooth), triangle, sawtooth (buzzy) or square (hollow).
  • Sweep — the shape the pitch wobble follows: sine (smooth wail), triangle, sawtooth (rising ramp) or square (sharp alternation, like a two-tone siren).
  • Pitch — the base frequency, shown in Hz.
  • Rate — how fast the sweep wobbles, in Hz. Tap (above) sets this for you.
  • Depth — how wide the sweep swings above and below the base pitch.
  • Spread — detunes the two internal oscillators apart, in cents. At 0 you get one pure tone; turned up, they beat against each other for a thicker, growling texture (used by the HORN preset).

Echo

  • Time — the delay between repeats, in milliseconds.
  • Repeats — how much of each echo feeds back into the next one, i.e. how long the tail rings out. This is what Feedback temporarily overrides while held.
  • Tone — a filter on the repeats only, darkening or brightening them without changing the dry siren.
  • Send — how much of the dry siren is fed into the echo in the first place.

Out

Volume — the master output level for everything.

Presets

Six built-in sounds: AIR RAID, POLICE, LASER, UFO, HORN and DUB. Each one is solved from real target frequencies and cycle times rather than guessed — see the comments above the PRESETS object in www/app.js for the exact numbers behind each one.

Hold one down and it sounds for as long as you hold it, the same gesture as the touch plate, so presets can be played, not just picked. Let go and it stops, unless Latch is on.

Mem

Three slots for your own settings, separate from the six built-in presets.

  • Hold a Mem button (about half a second) to save the current sound into that slot — it flashes gold and switches from "— 1 —" to "MEM 1" to show it's filled.
  • Tap a filled slot to instantly recall it.

Saved slots persist on the device and survive closing the app.

Layout

The oscilloscope, touch plate and transport row always stay pinned on screen and never scroll away. How the controls below them are arranged is up to you — three modes, chosen near the bottom of the screen, remembered on the device:

  • 1 · Stack — Siren, Echo and Out stacked in one scrolling column. The default.
  • 2 · Tabs — one section at a time behind Siren/Echo/Out tabs, everything fits with no scrolling.
  • 3 · Split — Siren and Echo side by side in two columns, dropping to one automatically when there isn't room.

Turn the phone sideways and the performance area (scope, plate, transport) moves to the left half of the screen with the controls on the right, so you can keep one finger on the plate while the other hand dials in a sound.

Status line

At the very bottom: the audio engine's current state and sample rate, a TEST TONE button to confirm sound is working, and the small feedback button described below.

Feedback & contact

Found a bug, have an idea, or just want to say hi? Two ways to reach us — both are also one tap away from inside the app itself, via the small envelope button on the status line.
