Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE YOU PUBLISH THIS]

Portable Dub Siren is a sound synthesizer. It is built to run entirely on your device, with no network connection. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no tracking of any kind. The app does not read your contacts, files, location, camera, or microphone, and asks for no permission that would let it.

What is stored on your device

The app saves a few things locally, using your browser or system's standard local storage, so your setup survives closing it:

  • The three memory slots — the synth settings you choose to save.
  • Your layout preference.
  • Your light or dark choice, if you overrode the system setting.

This never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing site data in your browser, deletes it permanently.

Purchases

Where the app is sold — a subscription or a one-time unlock on Google Play — the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Payment is made to Google, under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We never see or receive your card details, billing address, or any other payment information. What reaches the app is a yes-or-no answer about whether a purchase is active, which it keeps on the device alongside the settings above.

Network access

The Android app declares the INTERNET permission because the system component it is built on requires it. All sound is generated on the device and every file it needs is bundled inside the app, so it works with the network off. Where in-app purchases are available, the purchase and the check that it is still active are the only times the app talks to the network, and it talks only to Google Play.

This website

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and embeds nothing from third parties. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub records standard server request data — including IP addresses — for security and abuse prevention, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We do not have access to those logs beyond what GitHub shows us, and we do not combine them with anything else.

Children

The app is safe for all ages. Since it collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either.

Third parties

There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs in the app. The only third party involved at all is Google, in its role as the store that distributes the app and processes any purchase.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights over personal data held about you — access, correction, deletion, and others. We hold none, so in practice there is nothing for us to hand over or erase. For data Google holds about your purchases, use Google's own privacy controls. If you want to ask us anything regardless, write to PortableDubSiren@gmail.com.
